ABOUT THIS MODEL

Planet starting positions use approximate NASA/JPL orbital elements. The paths retain approximate eccentricities and inclinations, but planet sizes are enlarged, distances are compressed unevenly, and the Moon’s orbit is widened so the system fits on screen. Motion is accelerated and simplified; this is not a live ephemeris or a gravity simulation. Fact cards use measured values.
